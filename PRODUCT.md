# Kliseer product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Sales reps at US building-material and electrical-equipment suppliers/distributors.

## Product Purpose

Turn scattered business information into a reviewed company profile, targets, relevant prospects, and human-reviewed outreach drafts. Klise understands the seller; Seer surfaces prospects using dated public signals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Next.js app contains setup, multi-file draft extraction, saved prospects, rule-based scoring, sourced contacts, chat, and outreach drafts. Landing is static Astro at /; app is Next.js at /app on the same domain. Four distinct workflow demonstrations remain readable without animation or JavaScript. No automatic email sending, invented contacts, customer logos, testimonials, sales statistics, or pricing. Relevance is not buying intent. Harborline is a fictional seller study case.

## Brand Commitments

Official Kliseer SVG icon and wordmark, Inter, and existing semantic tokens. Landing uses the user-selected dark silver/violet Velvet Haze reference; internal app remains light. No literal weapon imagery or brand redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

Current app/components, design/kliseer.figcraft, design/export, design/tokens.css, docs/sources.md, scripts/seed-hunter-contacts.ts, three motion references, and background.md. Stale Seer Fit96 exports are not authoritative for current scores. Latest app navigation includes Contacts, not Profiling/ICP sidebar entries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable static HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion states, no scroll locking, and a loader tied only to critical above-fold readiness. Decorative background and lower-section animation load later and stop when hidden/offscreen. Assets failing must not trap visitors behind a loader.

## Authority

User approved design through implementation in this session. Existing landing-page-prompt.md and newer user loader constraints outrank older design-only status notes. This product record does not replace DESIGN.md or revise global app design decisions.
